AI Assistant

Say a messy request. It does it, step by step.

Give it one long sentence with typos and corrections. A planner rewrites it into clean steps, then it opens apps, taps, installs, sets volume and shows a live checklist while it works.

  • Tested with a 23-step request: 21 steps worked first try, the rest drove the fixes
  • Installs missing apps and asks for the right permission itself
  • Offline quick commands: flashlight, volume, timers, camera, settings
  • The AI part uses Groq: bring your own free key, stored only on your phone

  • 16 sensors
  • Speaker, microphone, vibration

Not supported yet

  • Calls, SMS, mobile dataNot tested without a SIM.

  • China-only HyperAI featuresNeed Xiaomi cloud services.

The journey

How a 32-bit phone got here

The short version. The full write-up lists every error, cause and fix.

Getting in: TWRPSamsung rejected every custom vbmeta until one byte (the flags, at offset 120) was changed in Samsung's own, keeping its signature trailer.
The 32-bit wallThe A13 ships a 32-bit system, but HyperOS 3 is 64-bit only. We built a hybrid vendor from the LineageOS Galaxy A21s (same Exynos 850 chip) plus 190 A13 files.
LineageOS 23.2 bootsAfter restoring lost file permissions and compiling kernel 4.19.325 ourselves.
HyperOS 3 bootsPatched Qualcomm-only graphics and media code to run on a Mali GPU and enabled XDP sockets in the kernel. Fifteen rounds to a stable boot.
Made usable64-bit-only apps, a touch fix that speaks Samsung's display protocol, OpenGL rendering and a ported camera shim.
An AI assistantBuilt from scratch: it plans long requests into steps and operates the phone.

Install

About 20 minutes

You need a Galaxy A13 4G SM-A135M (Exynos 850), an unlocked bootloader, a PC with Odin (Windows) or odin4 (Linux), and 7-Zip.

⚠ Beta: flash at your own risk. Flashing erases all data, so back up first. SELinux runs in permissive mode, which is less secure than stock, so don't use this phone for banking or sensitive work. For the SM-A135M only: other models can be bricked.
  1. Unlock the bootloader (Developer options → OEM unlocking, then the download-mode unlock). This wipes the phone.
  2. Download the files below and check them with SHA256SUMS. For HyperOS, put all three .7z parts in one folder, open the .001 with 7-Zip and extract: you get 2-hyperos-3.1-magisk.tar.
  3. Flash TWRP: in Download mode (Vol Up + Vol Down while plugging in USB) flash 1-twrp-vbmeta.tar in the AP slot.
  4. Format data: boot TWRP (Vol Down + Power until black, then Vol Up + Power) → Wipe → Format Data → type yes.
  5. Flash the ROM: back in Download mode, flash 2-hyperos-3.1-magisk.tar (or the LineageOS one) in AP.
  6. Boot and wait. You'll see the startup pages, then the HyperOS animation. The first boot takes a few minutes and apps get optimised once (a "Compiling apps" notification). Then install AIAssistant.apk and enable it in Accessibility.

Stuck? After three failed boots the phone goes to TWRP by itself. Flash again, or go back to Samsung stock firmware with Odin. Full guide: INSTALL.md.

FAQ

Questions

Will this work on my Galaxy A13 5G, A12 or other model?

No. It's built for the SM-A135M (A13 4G, Exynos 850) only. Other models use different hardware and can be bricked.

Can I go back to stock?

Yes. Flash Samsung's official firmware (A135MUBSDDZB3) with Odin in Download mode. That restores One UI; the unlocked bootloader stays unlocked.

Why is SELinux permissive?

During bring-up, the kernel logs security-policy violations instead of blocking them, which made the port possible. Making it enforcing needs every logged violation turned into a rule and re-tested. It's on the to-do list; until then this is a beta.

Why does it boot with those startup pages?

So you know what you are flashing: the credits, what works and what doesn't. They play in front of the normal HyperOS boot animation and add no time to the boot.

Does the AI Assistant send my data somewhere?

It sends what you type or say, plus a text description of the screen while it is working, to Groq using your own API key. Your key is stored only in the app's private storage. The offline quick commands never leave the phone.

Is this official or affiliated with Xiaomi or Samsung?

No. It's an unofficial community port. HyperOS is a trademark of Xiaomi and Galaxy of Samsung. See the credits below.

It doesn't boot, what now?

Wait up to 10 minutes on the first boot. If it fails, the phone reboots into TWRP on its own; re-flash the package. The write-up lists every boot failure we hit and its cause.
